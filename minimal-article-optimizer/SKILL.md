# Minimal Article Optimizer

Apply the 80/20 rule aggressively. Make only the smallest number of changes that produce the largest gains in search visibility, answer engine extractability, and generative engine citability.

## Core Workflow (run in order)

1. **Strip noise**  
   Remove all meta commentary, internal citation markers such as **[1]**, editorial notes, coverage notes, and excessive blank lines or inconsistent spacing. Preserve author voice and meaning.

2. **Standardize structure**  
   Ensure exactly one H1 title. Use logical H2 for major sections and H3 only when necessary. Short paragraphs (3-5 sentences max). Turn dense blocks into scannable lists or definition-style paragraphs where they improve extractability.

3. **80/20 visibility upgrades (do these, nothing more unless critical)**  
   - Place primary topic/keyword phrase naturally in the title, first paragraph, and one key heading.  
   - Add or refine one short "Key Points" or definition list near the top or conclusion (highest leverage for AEO/GEO).  
   - Ensure the core claim or stance appears clearly in the final paragraph.  
   - For any embedded image, generate one crisp alt text sentence focused on what the diagram shows and why it matters.  
   - Suggest 1 improved title variant and 1 meta description (under 160 characters) optimized for the primary intent.

4. **Output format**  
   Return:  
   - The full cleaned and minimally optimized markdown article  
   - A 3-5 bullet "Optimization Summary" listing exactly what was changed and why it improves visibility

## What NOT to do
- Do not rewrite sentences for style or add new content.  
- Do not add keyword stuffing or forced headings.  
- Do not expand into full SEO reports or long analyses. Keep it minimal.  
- Preserve the original author's tone, terminology, and series voice (e.g. Genesis Conductor framing).

## When to trigger
Use on any markdown essay or article draft that contains internal notations, meta text, spacing issues, or needs quick preparation for publication and maximum reach in search and AI answer systems. Especially effective for technical series on privacy architecture, consent, inference governance, and sovereign AI systems.
